Privacy

The game collects no personal information. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

Your campaigns, settings, rank, trophies and the record of your past games are stored only on your own device, in the game's local storage. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the game from your device deletes them.

If you export a save file or a save code, or share a picture of a result, that file goes only where you choose to send it.

If you tap Report a bug, and only then, the game sends us a report: the game version, your device type and screen size, your language and settings, your last moves in the game, any error messages, and, if you leave the box ticked, your current game. It contains no name, account, location or contact details, and is used only to fix the game.

The game's typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts the first time the game is opened with a connection; Google may log that request as it does for any web font. After that the game plays offline.

The game is suitable for general audiences. It depicts medieval warfare in a stylised way, without gore.

Support

For help, or to report a problem, open an issue at github.com/jdc001/crown-of-the-franks/issues. Say which version you are on (Settings shows it at the bottom) and what you were doing.
